## 🎯 Project Goal
To create a stunning, professional portfolio website for **TechSpark AI** that helps mid and small enterprises automate their marketing, HR, and finance operations using Gen AI. The goal is to get new clients to contact you.

## 🎨 Design & Look
- **Theme:** Cinematic, immersive, and highly premium (inspired by high-end tech hardware websites).
- **Colors:** Deep midnight blue/black background with soft, glowing gold and blue accents.
- **Layout:** "Floating" 3D elements, wide edge-to-edge glassmorphism cards, and a sleek modern flow based on the Aziro.com format.
- **Background:** A live, slow-moving "neural network" animation (golden glowing dots connecting with lines) to give a high-tech "things in motion" feel.
- **Fonts:** Modern, sharp, and clean (Outfit and Inter).

## 📄 Website Structure (Single Scrollable Page)

### 1. 🏠 Hero Section (Top of Page)
- **Title:** "AI-Native Engineering & Enterprise Solutions"
- **Subtitle:** "Transform your business with TechSpark's AI-native engineering. Build smarter products, scale innovation, and unlock growth."
- **Visuals:** 3D floating glass cards (like screens suspended in space) to give an immediate wow-factor.
- **Action:** A bright yellow button that says "Explore Services".

### 2. 🧩 AI Labs / Services
- Displayed as massive, wide cinematic cards with glowing lighting effects.
- **Services included:**

  - (More can be added as needed)

### 3. 📈 Impact / Outcomes
- Displayed as glowing statistics boxes.
- Showing metrics like "80% Reduction in manual workflows" and "5x Team growth acceleration".

### 4. 📞 Contact Section
- A premium, frosted-glass contact form for users to enter their Name, Email, and Message.
